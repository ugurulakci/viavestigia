The Water City
Cisterns, Aqueducts
& Hammam

Beneath Istanbul's skyline lies a water system that kept Constantinople alive for 1,500 years. This isn't a tour of monuments. It's a reading of the city's veins — underground, above the streets, in places most itineraries skip.

Underground cistern — a surviving part of the city's historic water network
Çemberlitaş Hamamı — Mimar Sinan, 1584
Grand Bazaar — Ottoman bath culture, still traded
Süleymaniye şadırvan — water ritual at the mosque terrace
Valens Aqueduct — Roman engineering, still standing
Zeyrek Çinili Hamamı — complimentary şerbet on the terrace
