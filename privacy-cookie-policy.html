Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to viavestigia.com

This page explains what data viavestigia.com actually collects, why, and what choices you have. It covers this website only. Our Substack publication (viavestigia.substack.com) and Viator, which handles bookings, run under their own privacy policies — linked below.

Who controls this data

Via Vestigia is operated by Uğur M. Ulakci, a Ministry-licensed tourist guide based in Istanbul, Turkey. For any privacy question or request, contact hello@viavestigia.com.

Analytics cookies — and your choice

We use two analytics tools to understand how people use this site: Google Analytics (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity. Neither is loaded, and neither sets any cookie, until you actively choose "Accept Analytics" in the cookie banner shown on your first visit. If you choose "Reject Non-Essential," these tools are never loaded on your device.

ServiceWhat it doesWhat it collects if accepted
Google Analytics 4 Standard traffic and behavior analytics Pages visited, approximate location (country/city level), device/browser type, referring site, session duration
Microsoft Clarity Session recording and heatmaps, so we can see how visitors actually use the pages Mouse movement, scroll behavior, clicks, and page interactions during your visit — recorded per session

Both are configured with advertising storage permanently set to "denied" — we do not use either tool for advertising, retargeting, or ad personalization, and neither tool is used to build advertising profiles.

Changing your choice later

You can open your cookie preferences at any time using the Cookie Preferences link in the footer of any page. Choosing "Reject Non-Essential" after previously accepting clears existing analytics cookies from your browser and reloads the page.

The newsletter

The "Subscribe to the Journal" link takes you directly to our Substack subscription page. We do not collect or process your email address on viavestigia.com — you enter it on Substack's own page, under Substack's own privacy policy. See Substack's Privacy Policy.

Booking

Bookings are currently completed through Viator, a third-party booking platform. When you click a "Book Now" or "Reserve" button, you leave viavestigia.com and complete your booking, including any payment and personal details, entirely on Viator's site under Viator's own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Via Vestigia does not currently offer direct booking through this website. Direct booking functionality is inactive and does not collect, store, or transmit personal data in its current state.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Basic technical access logs (such as IP address and browser type) may be retained by GitHub as part of standard hosting infrastructure, independent of the analytics tools described above. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

International data transfers

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are provided by companies based in the United States, so data collected through these tools (only after you accept) may be processed outside Turkey and the EEA. Both providers maintain their own compliance frameworks for international transfers.

Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, or to ask a question about this policy, contact hello@viavestigia.com.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if what the site actually collects changes — for example, if direct booking is activated, or a new analytics tool is added. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent change.
