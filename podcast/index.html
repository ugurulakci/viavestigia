Four Ways of Following a Trace

The series

World Capitals

Ancient capitals read as living civilizations, not ruins — Hattuşa, Tuşpa, and the seats of power that shaped Anatolia.

One Drink, One Place

Forgotten and half-remembered drinks — hardaliye, sübye — as living memory of a region's people and rituals.

World Stadiums

Ancient arenas as evidence of public life, competition, and belief — not just architecture.

One Artist, One Place

The craftspeople and sculptors behind individual monuments, and the places that made their work possible.

"Non terminus, sed ambulatio." Not a destination, but a walk.

The Via Vestigia Podcast is recorded and published in Turkish.
